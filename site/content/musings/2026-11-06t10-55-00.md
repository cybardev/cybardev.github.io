I think there is something profoundly _cute_ about the fact that one of the most common passwords is some variation of "I love you". It's actually inspiring and hope-inducing, that people reach for love for others, even when they're trying to secure something personal or private.

Sure it makes for a weak password, but there is an immense strength in being able to carry such love for others that it surfaces in your mind in seemingly unrelated situations.

---

PS: Please don't use any variation of "I love you" as your password. You can check this webpage (runs offline once loaded) to check the strength of your passwords: [lowe.github.io/tryzxcvbn](https://lowe.github.io/tryzxcvbn/)
