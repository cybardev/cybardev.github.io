In the dystopian present we are finding ourselves thrown into, there is no way to survive but to be delusionally hopeful - that better days are coming, that good things are happening, that there are good people doing them, and that we can each try our best to pull our weight in turning this ship around.

At the same time, we have to take care of ourselves. If you really don't have the energy to do something, that's okay. If you can, talk about it; if not actively, then at least when the topic comes up. Resist at every opportunity; every little pushback matters. If even that feels too draining, that's alright; it's not your fault the world is set up to feel that way. Do what you can, even if that is "just" sharing posts, or "just" being present to provide numbers, or "just" keeping the thought alive in the back of your head. Because, these "just"s lend themselves to justice, and the more people we have on board, the sooner we can build a better society.
